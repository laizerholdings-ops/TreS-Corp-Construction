ABOUT TRES CORP

ENGINEERING CONFIDENCE. BUILDING TOMORROW.

TreS Corp is a South African construction, building, renovation, and civil engineering company based in Vosloorus, Boksburg, Gauteng. We provide a complete, end-to-end range of structural building, civil groundworks, interior finishing, and exterior property development services for residential and commercial clients.

Every project is guided by quality workmanship, professional project execution, practical construction solutions, attention to detail, client satisfaction, and reliable project coordination—ensuring every structure is built to endure.
